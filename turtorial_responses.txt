Lesson 4: Add External Stylesheet
Question:
Why is an external stylesheet usually easier to maintain than many inline styles?

Student response:
Its easier to update an external stylesheet.  You just have to change one rule in the file and the change applies to every element, unlike internal which you have to change each element individually.
