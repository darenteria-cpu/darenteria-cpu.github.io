History

Go was developed by Google in the 2007 by Robert Griesemer, Rob Pike, and Ken Thompson.

Features

Go is a statically typed, compiled language designed for simplicity and efficiency.

Uses

Go is popular for building web services, cloud infrastructure, and concurrent applications.

It is also used in business intelligence and data analysis.
